**Role:** You are an expert college canteen manager, budget planner, and food business strategist.

**Context:** You have a total budget of ₹10,000 to improve a college canteen for one week. Your goal is to make the canteen affordable, attractive to students, and profitable while managing limited resources.

**Task:** Create a complete one-week canteen improvement plan covering:

1. **Food Menu:** Decide what food and beverages to offer, considering students' preferences, affordability, variety, and preparation time.
2. **Pricing:** Set reasonable selling prices for each item while covering costs and aiming to make a profit.
3. **Quantities:** Estimate how much of each item to prepare or purchase daily, avoiding both shortages and food wastage.
4. **Budget Allocation:** Divide the ₹10,000 budget between ingredients, packaging, basic improvements, and emergency expenses.
5. **Demand Management:** Explain how to handle rush hours, popular items selling out, changing demand, and long queues.
6. **Marketing:** Suggest creative, low-cost ways to attract students, such as combo meals, student discounts, daily specials, and promotions.
7. **One-Week Strategy:** Provide a day-by-day plan to introduce, monitor, and improve the canteen offerings based on student feedback and sales.

**Constraints:**
- Total budget must not exceed ₹10,000.
- The plan must cover exactly seven days.
- Food should be hygienic, fresh, and affordable for college students.
- Include realistic costs, quantities, and selling prices.
- Keep preparation and operations simple and manageable.

**Output Format:**
- A table showing the menu, quantity, cost price, selling price, and estimated earnings.
- A detailed ₹10,000 budget breakdown.
- A daily operational plan for all seven days.
- A demand-management and marketing strategy.
- A final financial summary estimating total expenses, revenue, and profit.

**Goal:** Design a practical, student-friendly canteen that maximizes value, minimizes food waste, handles demand efficiently, and makes the best possible use of the ₹10,000 budget.
